# 📊 Sales Analytics with AI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance and generate actionable business insights.

The dashboard combines traditional sales analysis with Power BI's AI-powered analytical capabilities to help explore regional performance, product categories, customer segments, sales patterns, and the factors influencing overall sales.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Compare sales across different regions
- Analyze product category performance
- Identify unusual sales patterns
- Discover factors influencing total sales
- Explore sales performance using interactive AI visuals
- Provide clear insights to support data-driven decision-making

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### Interactive Slicers
Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Product
- Region
- Category
- Customer Segment

### Total Sales by Region
The regional analysis compares sales performance across **East, South, West, and North**.

The East region records the highest total sales at approximately **62.5K**.

### Total Sales by Category
The category analysis compares sales across:

- Electronics
- Furniture
- Sports
- Clothing
- Food

Electronics is the leading category based on the dashboard results.

---

## 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis

### Anomaly Detection

The dashboard uses anomaly detection on the sales time series to highlight unusual changes and sales spikes, helping identify periods that may require further investigation.

### Key Influencers

The **Key Influencers** visual analyzes the factors associated with increases in Total Sales.

The dashboard indicates that **Electronics** is an important category associated with increased sales performance.

### Decomposition Tree

The **Decomposition Tree** enables interactive exploration of Total Sales across dimensions such as:

- Region
- Category

This makes it possible to drill into sales performance and understand how individual business dimensions contribute to the overall result.
